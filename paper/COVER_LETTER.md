We submit the enclosed manuscript for consideration as an Original Research Article in npj Quantum Information, with a preference for the open Collection "Quantum machine learning: understanding capabilities, limitations, and perspectives for quantum advantage."

**The scientific problem.** Zhao and Deng (npj Quantum Information, 2025) established a constant-versus-linear quantum learning advantage using an entanglement-assisted task where a quantum protocol with $2n$ preshared Bell pairs achieves perfect success with zero classical communication. They proved that communication-bounded classical models require $\Omega(n)$ bits for nontrivial success. However, the exact zero-error classical communication cost remained open.

**The advance.** We prove an exact reduction: the minimum one-way classical communication for perfect success equals $\lceil \log_2 f(n,3) \rceil$, where $f(n,3)$ is the ternary-cube covering number, equivalently the total domination number $\gamma_t(K_3^{\times n})$. Combining this with the 2026 covering asymptotic of Kuang and Wang yields the exact first-order rate $B^*(n,1) = n \log_2(3/2) + O(1)$, exposing nonadditive classical communication compression under parallel repetition.

**Why npj Quantum Information.** This work sits at the intersection of quantum machine learning, quantum communication, nonlocal game theory, and combinatorics---precisely the scope of npj Quantum Information and the target Collection. It provides rigorous, exact resource accounting that clarifies what quantum advantage does and does not mean in this setting, without overstating the scope.
